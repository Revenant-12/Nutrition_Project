6. Adaptive Activity & TDEE Rebalancing Engine (Weekly Update):
  a. Purpose:
     i. Automatically adjust baseline TDEE to reflect actual logged exercise volume rather than static user-selected activity tiers.
     ii. Avoid daily fluctuation by using a rolling average and applying adjustments only on a weekly schedule.


  b. Workout Energy Logging:
     i. When a workout session is ended:
        1. For Cardio: Estimate calories burned based on exercise type, duration, and user weight (or read from distance/time).
        2. For Strength Training: Estimate calories burned = (duration in minutes) * (user weight in kg * 0.06 kcal/min).
        3. Save workout calories to the user's daily workout history.


  c. Rolling Activity Evaluation (Evaluated every 7 days at 00:00 local time):
     i. Calculate Average Daily Workout Burn:
        1. Sum workout calories burned over the previous 14 days.
        2. Divide total by 14 to get Average Daily Exercise Burn.
     ii. Compute Calculated TDEE:
        1. Sedentary Baseline = BMR * 1.2
        2. Calculated TDEE = Sedentary Baseline + Average Daily Exercise Burn
     iii. Apply Smoothing / Boundary Limits:
        1. Compare Calculated TDEE against Current Baseline TDEE.
        2. Cap the change to a maximum of +/- 150 kcal per week to avoid drastic swings.
        3. Set New Baseline TDEE = Current Baseline TDEE + Clamped Difference.


  d. Macro & Target Re-allocation:
     i. Re-run Daily Target Calories = New Baseline TDEE + Goal Offset (e.g., -20% for weight loss, +300 kcal for gain).
     ii. Re-calculate gram targets for Protein, Fat, and Carbs using the updated Target Calories.
     iii. Display a notification to the user:
        "Based on your last 2 weeks of workouts, your daily baseline has been adjusted by [+/- X] calories to match your real activity level."
